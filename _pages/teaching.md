## Psych 240: Introduction to Cognitive Psychology

An introduction to the scientific study of the mind, covering perception, attention, memory, language, reasoning, and decision-making.

## Psych 302: Research Methods in Cognitive Neuroscience

Experimental design, data collection, and analysis in cognitive neuroscience, with hands-on training in neuroimaging and statistical methods.

## Psych 447: Neurochemical Basis of Behaviour, Mental Health, and Aging

How neurotransmitter systems shape behavior and cognition, their roles in mental health conditions, and how they change across the lifespan.

## Neuroimaging: Seeing the Brain in Action

Summer school for high school students introducing neuroimaging techniques and their application to understanding brain function. Part of the [Michigan Math and Science Scholars (MMSS)](https://lsa.umich.edu/mmss) program.
