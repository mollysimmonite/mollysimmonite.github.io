## University of Michigan, Department of Psychology

**Adjunct Lecturer**

- **Psych 240: Introduction to Cognitive Psychology** — An introduction to the scientific study of the mind, covering perception, attention, memory, language, reasoning, and decision-making.
- **Psych 302: Research Methods in Cognitive Neuroscience** — Experimental design, data collection, and analysis in cognitive neuroscience, with hands-on training in neuroimaging and statistical methods.
- **Psych 447: Neurochemical Basis of Behaviour, Mental Health, and Aging** — How neurotransmitter systems shape behavior and cognition, their roles in mental health conditions, and how they change across the lifespan.

**Michigan Math and Science Scholars (MMSS)**

- **Neuroimaging: Seeing the Brain in Action** — Summer school for high school students introducing neuroimaging techniques and their application to understanding brain function.
